# Privacy Policy — Seclusa Notes

**Effective: 2026-11-06**

Seclusa Notes is a static progressive web app. It is provided as-is at no cost.

## What the app collects

**Nothing is uploaded.** The app does not create accounts, send analytics, set tracking cookies, or upload your notes.

Notes and labels stay in IndexedDB in this browser. Theme, background, and the date of your last backup stay in localStorage on this device. The service worker cache holds only the app shell (HTML, CSS, scripts, icons, fonts), not note text.

Optional reminder notifications require your explicit permission in the browser. Encrypted backups are written only to files you choose to download or share; the password never leaves your device.

The service worker may revalidate same-origin app shell files when you are online. It does not transmit note contents.

This policy may be updated by posting a new version in this file.
